# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 4 September 2026**

Quotology is made by Kshitij Koranne (KJR Labs), Vadodara, India.

## What Quotology collects

**Nothing.** There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and
no tracking. Quotology does not have a server, so there is nowhere for your
data to be sent.

## What stays on your phone

Your saved quotes, your chosen topics, your text-size setting and your daily
notification setting are stored on the device only. Deleting the app deletes
them. They are never uploaded.

## What leaves your phone

To show fresh quotes, the app requests them from these third-party services:

- ZenQuotes — zenquotes.io
- The Quotes Hub — thequoteshub.com
- Bhagavad Gita API — vedicscriptures.github.io
- Quotable — api.quotable.kurokeita.dev
- Stoic Quotes — stoic.tekloon.net
- DummyJSON — dummyjson.com

Like any web request, these carry your device's IP address, and one of them
also carries the name of the topic being requested (for example `wisdom`).
That is all. No identifier for you or your device is attached, and no request
contains anything you have saved or written.

These services are not operated by us and have their own privacy practices.
We do not receive any data back about you from them, and we keep no logs.

## Permissions

- **Notifications** — only if you turn on the daily quote. Used to show one
  quote each morning on your own device. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- **Add to Photos** — only when you tap Save on a poster. The app can add the
  image you chose to your photo library. It cannot read your existing photos;
  it never asks for that access.

## Children

Quotology is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect
information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it and the new version
appears here.
