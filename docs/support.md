## Common questions

**The quotes are the same every time I open the app.**
Quotology picks one set per day so there is a shared "today". Pull the Refresh
button on the home screen for a new set at any time.

**I only want quotes on one subject.**
Settings → Your topics. Every set, and every refresh, stays inside what you
pick. Choose one topic if you want only that one.

**The daily quote never arrives.**
Check that notifications are allowed for Quotology in your phone's system
settings, then toggle the daily quote off and on again in Settings.

**Where do the quotes come from?**
Settings → Attributions lists every source, translation and typeface.

**A quote is misattributed, or you have used something of mine.**
Email the address above with the quote and I will correct or remove it in the
next release.

## Reporting a problem

It helps to include your phone model, your OS version, and what you were doing
when it went wrong.
